

## Hair Salon Demo Website — Nordic Luxury

A stunning, professional hair salon website template that demonstrates your web development capabilities. Potential clients should see this and immediately want one for their own salon.

### Design Direction: "Nordic Luxury"
- **Colors**: Deep volcanic blacks, glacier blues, warm gold accents, crisp whites
- **Typography**: Elegant serif headings (Playfair Display), clean sans-serif body text
- **Feel**: High-end, spa-like, premium — think luxury Reykjavik salon

### Pages & Sections

#### 1. **Hero / Landing**
- Full-screen background image with subtle overlay
- Salon name, tagline ("Where Nordic Beauty Meets Artistry")
- Prominent "Book Now" CTA button
- Smooth scroll navigation

#### 2. **Services & Pricing**
- Elegant card layout with service categories (Cut & Style, Color, Treatments, Bridal)
- Prices displayed clearly per service
- Hover animations for polish

#### 3. **Photo Gallery**
- Masonry-style grid of hairstyle photos
- Lightbox modal on click
- Filter by category (Color, Cuts, Bridal, etc.)

#### 4. **Team / Stylists**
- Stylish profile cards with photos, names, specialties
- Hover effect revealing bio
- 3-4 sample stylists with placeholder photos

#### 5. **Instagram Feed Section**
- Simulated Instagram-style grid showing client transformation photos
- "Follow us on Instagram" CTA
- Styled to look like an embedded feed with likes/comments indicators

#### 6. **Online Booking**
- Step-by-step booking form: Select Service → Choose Stylist → Pick Date/Time → Confirm
- Clean multi-step UI (demo only, no real backend)
- Confirmation screen with booking summary

#### 7. **About / Story**
- Brief salon story with atmospheric photo
- Icelandic-inspired brand narrative

#### 8. **Footer**
- Location with embedded map placeholder
- Opening hours
- Contact info & social links
- "Websites like this from only 19,990 ISK/month" subtle branding line

### Interactions & Polish
- Smooth scroll animations on section entry
- Sticky navigation header with transparent-to-solid transition
- Mobile-responsive throughout
- Subtle parallax effects on hero and about sections
